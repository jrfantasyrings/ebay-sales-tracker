Privacy Policy

eBay Sales Tracker is a private application used to retrieve and organize eBay sales and transaction information for the account owner.

Information obtained from eBay is used only for sales tracking, accounting, and business reporting purposes.

eBay user information is not sold, shared, rented, or provided to third parties.

Information is retained only as necessary for business recordkeeping and is handled in accordance with applicable eBay requirements.

Requests relating to stored eBay account information, including deletion requests, are processed in accordance with applicable eBay requirements.
